The flow

Who does what at each pipeline stage

The stages (Pre-leads, Pricing…) are not tabs of the app. They are the Supply Opportunity pipeline stages, i.e. where the purchase stands. HubSpot shows them out of the box: in the record header and as columns of the Supply Opportunities board (what the list tabs were in Bidmonitor). In our design they only change what the user can do at each point: for example, "Create proposal" is only enabled once PM prices exist. The tabs we do add to the record are Vehicle information and Proposal.

11

Reviews, sends and edits the proposal

  1. Once created, the proposal is a Draft. The purchaser reviews it through the link on the record.
  2. Sends it to the supplier: "Copy link" to share it via WhatsApp or their own email, or "Send by email" from HubSpot with the link included. Then clicks "Mark as sent" and it becomes Sent.
  3. If something must change, clicks "Edit proposal" to edit it: the same modal opens prefilled. The link is always the same: the supplier sees the changes once they're saved. If it was Sent, it stays Sent and the supplier is told with "Send again by email".
DesignUS-04
Statuses: only Draft and Sent. The button is called "Edit proposal" (in Bidmonitor, "Adjust proposal") because it modifies the existing proposal. Email keeps a tracked record of the link; customers often use WhatsApp.
One sent proposal, the same link and no version history in HubSpot (BAS World, 6 Oct).
Sending: copy the link, with an option to email it from HubSpot. Either way it's marked with "Mark as sent": the app isn't told whether the email went out.
Open: the status of a sent proposal that is edited (proposal: it stays Sent) and where the supplier's answer is recorded.

Step by step

The same flow, in order

  1. Stage 1 · Pre-leads
  2. 0The supplier offers a vehicleIt comes in through several channels: trade-ins (vehicles handed in when buying a new one; added by sales), incoming calls (Elevate service), email, WhatsApp (if the supplier forwards a message to BAS World's WhatsApp, the vehicle is created automatically), tickets (someone fills in a form) or manual search.
  3. 1The purchaser has a Supply Opportunity record in HubSpotUsually the purchaser creates it and fills in the fields by hand: linked supplier (company and contact), source and lead generator. WhatsApp leads arrive already created; the purchaser reviews and completes them. Documents and remarks sit at Supply Opportunity level, using native attachments and notes. This is the native record: header, columns and associations come with HubSpot.
  4. 2Adds one or more vehiclesIn the "Vehicle information" tab: a vehicle table + "Add vehicle". Picking a type shows only that type's fields. Brand → model → cabin are chained. Each vehicle, or each bundle of identical vehicles, is a line item. A bundle has a quantity and a unit price (5 × €20,000 = €100,000); its units differ only in mileage (or hours), license plate and VIN. The license plate is stored. Nice-to-have: fill in data from the license plate (Dutch registry only).
  5. 3Uploads photosPer vehicle, up to 10. Each vehicle has its own slider, so the user picks which vehicle's photos to see. The slider uses native components: large photo, arrows, "3 / 10" counter, thumbnail row and zoom in a modal. The first photo is the main one and can be changed with "Set as main photo". Photos aren't reordered, so no iframe is needed.
  6. Stage 2 · Pricing
  7. 4Asks Product Management for a priceWith the supplier's prices filled in (asking, proposed sales, offer; per unit), the purchaser clicks "Ask for price" on each vehicle or bundle and picks the urgency: not urgent (24 h), urgent today (before the end of the day) or urgent now (immediately). It can't be changed afterwards. If urgent, the vehicle goes to the top of PM's list in Bidmonitor. The Supply Opportunity moves to Pricing.
  8. 5The request goes to BidmonitorThe developer's backend sends the vehicle data and prices. We don't design this, but we do design the "request sent" state.
  9. 6PM sets the price constraintsMax offer price, estimated selling price range (min/max), minimum auction price, prefinance amount and pricing strategy. PM keeps working in Bidmonitor (or a separate pricing tool). Prices come in through the integration and aren't changed in HubSpot. For purchaser–PM communication, HubSpot @mentions for now.
  10. 7The answer comes back to HubSpotSaved as vehicle properties; the Supply Opportunity moves to Price available. PM takes 24 hours at most: we design a visible waiting state and a notice to the purchaser when the price arrives.
  11. Stage 3 · Price available
  12. 8The purchaser sees the constraintsIn the pricing card, next to the supplier's prices.
  13. 9Creates the proposalOne proposal per vehicle or bundle. A modal prefilled with PM's data. Adds expected days to sell, logistics and workshop costs, a price explanation and the language. Picks which offers are included (direct purchase, commission sale, auction), with amounts, and which is recommended: ticked = included, unticked = left out. The modal is the simplified version: solutions visible, the rest folded.
  14. 10Generated with the existing APIThe same one Bidmonitor uses today. It calculates commission and inspection and returns a URL.
  15. Stage 4 · Proposal sent · Propuesta enviada
  16. 11Reviews, sends and edits the proposalOnce created, the proposal is a Draft and its link appears on the record; the button becomes "Edit proposal". The purchaser reviews and sends it: "Copy link" to share it via WhatsApp or their own email, or "Send by email" to open HubSpot's email with the link included. Then clicks "Mark as sent" and it becomes Sent. Those are the only two statuses. "Edit proposal" is for editing it: the same modal opens with the current data. The link is always the same and no versions are kept: the supplier sees the changes once they're saved. If it was Sent, it stays Sent and the supplier is told with "Send again by email".
  17. 12The supplier picks a solutionOn Basworld's public page, which we don't build. Their choice doesn't come back to HubSpot: it's communicated to the purchaser and handled manually.
  18. 13ClosingIf accepted, the purchase order is created. Today that's done manually in the ERP; "Create purchase" in HubSpot is phase 2. If not, it's closed with a reason.
