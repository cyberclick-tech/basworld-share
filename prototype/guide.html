Prototype guide · vehicle purchasing in HubSpot

1. What this is

It is a prototype that simulates a Purchase record inside HubSpot. It shows how the app would behave before it is built. It is not the real HubSpot: nothing is saved, no email is sent, and the data (vehicles, prices, license plates) is made up.

The record has two tabs of ours: Vehicle information (vehicles, photos and pricing) and Proposal (proposals). Everything else (header, stage, contacts, attachments) is HubSpot's own and we haven't redesigned it.

2. Finding your way around

  1. The record (centre of the screen): click a vehicle to see its photos, pricing and details. «‹ All vehicles» takes you back to the table.
  2. Prototype controls (bottom right, dark box): not part of HubSpot. It is your remote control to try things without having to trigger them. Open and close it with the arrow.
  3. Pink bar at the top: «↺ Reset demo» puts everything back to the start. The other two switches are for the developer.
  4. For the developer (you can ignore this): «Component names» shows pink labels with the HubSpot component of each block, and «Accessibility notes» shows blue labels with accessibility notes. Both start off; switch them on only if you need them.
  5. Tips: when you click a scenario, a green box appears above the panel saying what to do. Close it with the ×.

If anything looks odd, click «↺ Reset demo».

4. Two things we need you to choose

At the bottom of «Prototype controls» there are two selectors that change the design. Try both options of each and tell us which works better (they are questions C32 and C33 below):

  • Identical vehicles: A · Quantity (a single line item with a quantity; mileage and VIN per unit) or B · Duplicate (each unit is a row; «Duplicate» copies everything except mileage and VIN). Note: in B each unit is its own line item, so each would have its own price request and its own proposal (5 identical vehicles = 5 requests and 5 proposals); in A, a whole bundle has just one. The prototype flags this with a yellow notice when you choose B.
  • Proposal modal: Full (as in Bidmonitor, in two columns) or Simplified (just the solutions, with costs folded away).

The other selectors (Vehicles table, Proposal tab) are only there to show the states: with data, empty, loading and error.

5. What we need you to confirm

We made some decisions ourselves so the design works. They are in the panel, under «Our proposals · to confirm» (open it with the arrow): each line takes you to the screen where it shows. Here they are as questions:

No.Question
C22Can a price be requested for a WhatsApp vehicle before its details are complete? Right now the button is disabled until then.
C23For each type we used your required and optional fields (spreadsheet of 30/09). We also made the asking price required and folded the optional fields. Is that right?
C24The license plate is used to look up the details. Should it also be stored as a vehicle field?
C25Once a price has been requested, should the purchaser be able to change the urgency or cancel the request?
C26What is the difference between «Urgent today» and «Urgent now» for PM? Are both needed?
C27If the supplier changes their price after PM has answered, should the purchaser be able to ask for a price again?
C28Proposal rules: at least one solution included, the recommended one must be included, and the amounts of the included solutions are required. Is that right? Are any other fields required?
C29When a proposal becomes Sent, should the Purchase stage change by itself (for example to «Proposal sent»)? With several proposals, we suggest it changes only when all of them are Sent, not with the first one. Is that right?
C30When a sent proposal is edited, does the supplier keep the same link (which then shows the latest version) or get a new link per version? We assumed one link per version.
C31Bundles and fields: (1) in a bundle each unit has mileage and VIN; in construction equipment and agriculture, working hours and VIN; in semi trailers, only the VIN. Is that right? (2) Power, mileage and working hours allow «Don't know» in your form; here they don't, because they are required. Should they? (3) What unit does «Capacity» use in Truck and Semi trailer?

6. What is not real

  • The data is made up and nothing is saved: reloading the page or clicking «Reset demo» puts everything back.
  • PM's prices, the license plate registry, sending emails and «Copy link» are simulated.
  • Uploading photos opens HubSpot's native file picker; here only a message appears.
  • The public proposal page (proposal.basworld.com) is not part of this design: «Open v1 ↗» only says that it would open.
  • The look is «similar, not exact» to HubSpot: what matters is where each piece goes and how it behaves.

7. Words we use

PurchaseThe whole purchase from a supplier (the record).
Line itemOne vehicle, or a bundle of identical vehicles. Each one has its own price and proposal.
BundleA batch of identical vehicles: quantity and price per unit (5 × €20,000 = €100,000).
PMThe pricing team. They keep working in Bidmonitor; here we only see their answer.
Draft · SentThe two statuses of a proposal.
